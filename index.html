HER2 IHC reader study

Score each case's HER2 immunohistochemistry as you would in routine practice (ASCO/CAP 2018, affirmed 2023). Each case consists of one or more regions (ROIs) from the same tumour. Review all ROIs, then give one score for the case.

Scores are saved automatically in this browser on this computer. Please use the same computer and browser for a whole session, and export the CSV when you finish each session (it is also offered automatically at the end). Send the CSV files to the study coordinator.

Coordinator: scores on the study server

Only you, the owner, can see this panel. Each reader writes only their own record.

Scoring guide

Assess invasive carcinoma only (not in-situ carcinoma or normal ducts). Use the H&E on the left to locate invasive tumour. Score membrane staining in the HER2 IHC on the right, across all ROIs of the case together, by the percentage of invasive tumour cells.

0No staining, or incomplete, faint/barely perceptible membrane staining in ≤10% of invasive tumour cells.
1+Incomplete, faint/barely perceptible membrane staining in >10% of tumour cells.
2+Weak to moderate complete membrane staining in >10% of tumour cells (equivocal).
3+Circumferential, complete, intense membrane staining in >10% of tumour cells.

Images are shown at native resolution (about 20×). You can zoom to about 40× digitally. Some IHC images in this study are real and some are computer-generated from the H&E. For each case, choose one score. Choose Cannot be scored only if the images do not allow a HER2 score.

Controls: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, double-click to zoom in, Home button to fit. Pan/zoom of the two panels is linked (untick "Link panels" to move them separately).
